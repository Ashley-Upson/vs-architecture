# Exposure handoffs

[Catalogue](readme.md)

## Condition

A broker may call an exposure to begin another stack. A known earlier service layer bypassing remaining categories to call an exposure is a violation.

## Verification

Test an unmet condition, its adjustment or diagnostic, and an unchanged second application. Test applicability separately from other diagram types.
