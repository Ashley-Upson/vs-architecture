# Parent centring

[Catalogue](readme.md)

## Condition

A parent is centred between the outer edges of its exclusively owned child set.

## Allowed changes

Parent X.

## Applicability and precedence

Parents offset to preserve vertical passages are exempt.

## Verification

Exercise an unmet condition, assert the stated outcome, then apply the rule again and assert no further meaningful change. Integration checks must also assert that neighbouring rules do not undo the outcome.
