# Unclassified links

[Catalogue](readme.md)

## Condition

Composition, inheritance and unknown-category links have no architectural violation verdict.

## Verification

Test an unmet condition, its adjustment or diagnostic, and an unchanged second application. Test applicability separately from other diagram types.

[UnclassifiedLinkLayoutRuleProcessingService](../../StandardIo.ArchitectureDiagram.Core2/Services/Processings/Layout/UnclassifiedLinkLayoutRuleProcessingService.cs)

## Applicability

Architecture structural links or links involving an unknown category.

## Allowed changes

IsLayerViolation only.
